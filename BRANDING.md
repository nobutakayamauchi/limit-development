## Meaning

WEB OPEN FOUNDRY is the public, participatory operating surface that combines:

- COMPANY — human responsibility, priorities, approvals and operating decisions
- LABORATORY — requests, pain points, research and validation
- FACTORY — implementation, dogfood, repair and product development
- SHOP — products and services that survived the development process
- DAILY HISTORY — one durable public-safe record per day

Core loop:

`REQUEST / PAIN -> RESEARCH -> PROTOTYPE -> DOGFOOD -> PRODUCT -> SHOP -> NEW EVIDENCE`
